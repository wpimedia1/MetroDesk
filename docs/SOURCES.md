# Third-party sources

The recorded runs in `records/` (and the read-only demo built from them) contain text retrieved from public web pages through Tavily search and extract during each run, quoted as evidence for the agents' citations. Copyright in that text stays with its authors and publishers; it is not covered by this repository's MIT license.

Text is kept only where it is clearly reusable: works of the US federal government, statute and regulation text, and text under an open license. For every other source the text was deleted from the records after the runs and replaced by a notice; the citation, the agents' short quoted passages and the URL remain. Those sources are marked "text removed" below.
